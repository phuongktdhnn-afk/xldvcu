XLDV 2026 - BAN CHAY DON GIAN TREN WINDOWS
==========================================

1. Cai Docker Desktop:
   https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop/

2. Mo Docker Desktop va cho den khi Docker chay.

3. Giai nen thu muc XLDV_2026_Web_Simple vao may.

4. Double-click file:
   START_XLDV.bat

5. Cho he thong khoi dong. Trinh duyet se tu mo:
   http://localhost:4000

- Admin: admin / admin123

DUNG HE THONG:
- Double-click STOP_XLDV.bat

NEU BI LOI:
- Double-click XEM_LOI.bat
- Gui lai noi dung man hinh cho nguoi phu trach.

DU LIEU:
- Excel mau nam trong thu muc data.
- Du lieu van hanh duoc luu trong PostgreSQL/Docker volume.
- Khong xoa volume neu chua sao luu du lieu.

Luu y: ban nay dung Docker Desktop de giu viec cai dat don gian va on dinh.
